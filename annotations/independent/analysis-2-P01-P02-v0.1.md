## Cross-episode comparison

1. AI refinement is stronger and more extensive in Episode 2.
2. Episode 1 contains more early AI-provided conceptual framing; Episode 2 starts from a user-originated architectural proposal.
3. Episode 2 has greater co-construction and interdependence.
4. Episode 1 is more analytical/reclassificatory; Episode 2 is constructive/design-oriented.
5. The major methodological ambiguity is conceptual authorship: when a user gives an intuition and the AI supplies the formal system, attribution should not collapse origin and development into one variable.
